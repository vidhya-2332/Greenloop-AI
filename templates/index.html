Safety First

When NOT to Upcycle: Safe Disposal Guidance

Not all waste can be safely reused. Follow these environmental safety protocols when upcycling is hazardous.

Hazardous & Chemical Containers

Never upcycle containers that held pesticides, automotive oils, paint thinners, or harsh industrial acids into household planters or food storage.

Protocol: Deliver to specialized municipal hazardous waste collection depots.

Broken & Chipped Glassware

Shattered glass poses serious injury risks to sanitation workers and cannot be recycled with regular container glass due to different melt points.

Protocol: Wrap securely in multiple layers of newspaper, tape firmly, and label "DANGER: BROKEN GLASS".

Contaminated Paper & Pizza Boxes

Cardboard soaked in food oil or cheese cannot be re-pulped in paper recycling facilities because oil prevents paper fiber bonding.

Protocol: Cut away clean ungreased sections for recycling; compost non-printed oily parts in compost pits.
Academic & Technical Disclosure

System Architecture & AI Disclosure

In college and academic evaluations, honesty regarding machine learning integration is paramount. Here is exactly how GreenLoop AI functions under the hood:

1. Image Verification (Pillow)

Pillow checks that an uploaded file is a supported, readable image. This validates the file itself, not the waste material shown in its pixels.

2. Local Visual Suggestion

A local waste-trained MobileNetV2 model analyzes the photo pixels and suggests one of the supported material streams. Results are suggestions, so please confirm them. If the model is unavailable, GreenLoop uses clearly labelled description or filename hints instead.

3. No image-recognition claim

A waste-specific MobileNetV2 model is included for offline photo suggestions. It works best when one waste item is clear, centered, and fills a well-lit photo. It can still make mistakes; please confirm the result before following disposal guidance.

4. User-entered SQLite activity log

The dashboard summarizes activities users record in the local SQLite database. These records are not independently verified, and points are gamification only; the app does not calculate carbon savings.
